# SQL Mastery — Module 5: ctes

Total questions: **20**

Use the included SQLite database to solve the questions.

## Beginner

1. Use a CTE to calculate the total revenue for each order.

2. Use a CTE to identify orders with revenue above the average order revenue.

3. Use a CTE to calculate the total revenue generated by each customer.

4. Use a CTE to identify high-value customers based on total revenue of at least 1000.

5. Use a CTE to calculate total revenue for each product.

## Easy

10. Use a CTE to identify product categories with revenue above the average category revenue.

## Intermediate

## Advanced
